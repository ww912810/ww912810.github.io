Why people self-host their files

Cloud drives throttle your downloads, scan your uploads and charge you every month, forever. A file station on hardware you own has none of that.

⚡

No speed limits

On your home network it runs at full LAN speed. Large videos and backups move in minutes, not hours.

🔒

Actually private

Files never leave your house unless you share them. No ads, no scanning, no account bans deleting your archive.

👥

Accounts & permissions

An admin account for you, read-only guest accounts for family or teammates — each folder can have its own rules.

📱

Any device, any browser

Phone, tablet, laptop — if it has a browser, it can browse, stream and upload. Drag-and-drop, resumable uploads.

🖥

Runs on almost anything

An old laptop, a mini PC, a NAS, a cheap VPS. If it still turns on, it can probably be your file station.

FAQ

What computer do I need?

Almost any PC, laptop, NAS or VPS that can run Python — including old machines. In our own test the station used about 62 MB of RAM. If it turns out your device is incompatible, we refund in full.

How does the guided setup work if you can't access my computer?

Exactly by design: we never touch your machine. We prepare every file and configuration in advance, then sit with you in a live chat session while you run the few steps yourself, checking each result as you go. Most setups finish within an hour.

Can I reach my files from outside my home?

Yes — the manual covers safe options such as a mesh VPN, or port forwarding with the risks explained. Tell us your situation during setup and we will configure the sensible route.

Why crypto only?

We are a small studio selling worldwide without a payment platform taking a cut or holding funds. USDT/USDC on Polygon or BSC keeps fees near zero. Every order is verified on-chain before delivery.

Is the software really free?

Yes. The station is built on copyparty, an open-source project under the MIT license, and we include the license in your pack. You are paying for our configuration, guidance and support.
